## Full description (Play "Full description", Apple "Description", 4000 max)

```
Long ago, every age of the world was bound by one thread of golden light. Then the thread broke, and its relics scattered across Jambudvīpa. One crowned king sets out to bring them home.

Odyssey of the Ages is a fast, colourful endless runner through legendary worlds. Swipe to change lanes, jump over gaps, slide under fallen arches and collect gold mudra coins as you race toward the last city of legend.

SEASON 1: JAMBUDVĪPA, TEN WORLDS
• Ayodhya Run: lantern-lit palace streets of the royal city
• Ganga Run: sunrise ghats, boats and floating planks across the river
• Thar Desert Run: blazing dunes, sand traps and rolling boulders
• Himalayan Run: ice crevasses, rope bridges and snowy monasteries
• Vijayanagar Run: granite bazaars and toppling palace pillars
• Dragon Valley: a fantasy gorge of fire and stone
• Lanka Run: a golden palace and its moat of floating platforms
• Indus Run: the brick cities of an ancient river valley
• Dwarka: The Sunken Kingdom: coral ledges in a deep sea trench
• Dwarka Run: The Final Legend: the golden city and the final bridge

FEATURES
• Hand-painted 3D-style worlds, each with its own art, music and obstacles
• A royal outfit for the king in every world
• Daily rewards, missions and upgrades to power up your runs
• Revive with the coins you earn: no ads, no purchases, ever
• Simple one-thumb swipe controls
• Plays offline, no account needed
• Available in English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi and Tamil

Lace up, Your Majesty. Your odyssey begins now.
```

### Apple (Age rating)

Answer **None** to every content row (cartoon or fantasy violence, realistic violence, horror, mature themes, profanity, sexual content, alcohol/drugs, simulated gambling, medical) and **No** to unrestricted web access, user-generated content, messaging and contests. Not "Made for Kids". Expected rating: **4+**.

### Play › Target audience and content

Age groups: **9–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18+**. The game is child-friendly and already meets the Families policy (no ads, no ad SDKs, no data collection, no purchases), so the listing is declared suitable for children.
