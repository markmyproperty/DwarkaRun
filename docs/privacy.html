Odyssey of the Ages: Privacy Policy

MMYP Technologies · Effective 28 November 2026 · Updated 28 November 2026

Odyssey of the Ages ("the game") is made by MMYP Technologies (OPC) Private Limited ("MMYP Technologies", "we", "us"), India. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the game on Android, iOS or the web.

The short version

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. The only time it goes online is a quick check with Google Play or the App Store for a newer version of the game.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device only: coins and gems earned in the game, the worlds you have finished, your best distance, missions, daily reward streak, chosen hero outfit, and sound and music settings. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the game, or clearing its data, removes it.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any device identifier, and it does not send any data to us or to third parties.

Update check

When the game starts, and when you return to it, it asks the store it was installed from whether a newer version is available. On Android this uses Google Play's in-app update service; on iPhone it asks Apple's public App Store lookup service for the game's latest version number. The request contains only the game's app ID. If a newer version exists, the game asks you to update before you keep playing. We do not receive or store anything from this check; Google and Apple handle it under their own privacy policies.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social SDKs. The app stores (Google Play and the Apple App Store) may collect information when you download the game under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

In-game currency

Coins and gems are earned only by playing. They cannot be bought with real money and have no value outside the game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles information (for example by adding online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
